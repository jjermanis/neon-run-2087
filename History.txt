Version 01.01	35 minutes dev	2 minutes CPU	3 minutes test		40 minutes

This time, I wanted to make a game that looked like it was being played on a
Commodore 64 computer. I asked ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for ideas. I like
one that ChatGPT had the best: 
"You're a courier racing through a futuristic city to deliver a mysterious 
data capsule before the city security forces catch you."
I liked the theme. It seemed the most inline with a C64 game that would be
fun and feasible. I worked with ChatGPT on a prototype... and it did not go
well. Generally UI was good. Playing had many errors around it. I took the
same game model, and asked Claude to create a prototype. I shouldn't be
surprised with Claude - but it did a much better than expected prototype.
Don't get me wrong: there are defects for sure. But for a Proof of Concept?
Perfect.
I've documented some basic next steps to proceed from here.


Version 01.02	18 minutes dev	1 minute CPU	1 minute test		1 hour

In my initial testing, I only saw one significant flaw. When you turn in the
game, the road visually turns to the left and right. When the game is over,
and the car stops... you'd still see the road move. It would also happen in 
game if you got the speed down to 0: the road would be still going forward
but would move turn left and right.
I asked Claude to resolve. It was basing road turns passed on time. It
switched to using distance. This was fairly east to resolve.


Version 01.03	9 minutes dev	0 minutes CPU	1 minute test		1 hour 10

Playing, I thought the lanes were a little thin. I checked with Claude: cars
take up 67% (aka 2/3rds) of a lane. Standard roads is 50%, and narrow roads
are 60%. I switched to narrow roads for this: more reasonable, for UI and
playability.
Claude did a balanced solution: the road is a little wider, and the car is a
little more narrow. A small change, yet light impact visually, but plays a
bit easier.
